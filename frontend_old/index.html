FAQ

Common patient questions

Can I book an appointment and still join the queue?

Yes. Online appointments generate a token automatically and enter the same priority queue used for walk-in patients.

How are emergency patients handled?

Emergency cases receive the highest priority. Within the same priority group, Medique serves patients in first-in, first-out order.

Can I see my live position?

Yes. Use Queue Tracking on this site or sign in to the portal to see your token, estimated waiting time, and doctor assignment.

Appointment Booking

Reserve a visit and receive a queue token

The system validates doctor availability, prevents duplicate bookings, blocks overlapping slots, and inserts the patient into the priority FIFO queue.

Queue Rules

Priority Queue + FIFO

Serving order is Emergency, High Priority, then Normal. Patients inside the same priority level are served by booking time.
